THE HELPFUL LITTLE THINGS

At home, informed.

Practical resources for a smoother everyday.

Your move-in checklist

Confirm your move-in date and key pickup with your management team. Arrange utilities, update your mailing address, photograph the condition of your home, and keep a copy of your signed lease.

Taking care of your home

Test smoke alarms regularly, keep air vents clear, and report water leaks promptly. Follow your lease and appliance manuals for maintenance responsibilities.

Payments & lease questions

Review your signed lease for rent due dates, approved payment methods, and any applicable fees. Contact your property manager to verify payment instructions before sending money.

Planning your next chapter

Check your lease for renewal and notice requirements. Contact management in advance to discuss renewal options or schedule move-out steps.
