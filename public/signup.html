How your account information is used

Your name and email identify your account and allow management to support your tenancy. Agents and administrators can manage your account and review applications you submit. You can see only your own lease, rent records, documents, maintenance requests, and messages.

Creating an account does not approve an application, reserve a home, create a lease, or collect payment. Tenant sign-up does not grant agent or administrator access. Do not include card numbers, bank passwords, or identity documents in messages.

Contact management through the portal for account help. Email password recovery is not configured; an administrator can assist with access.
