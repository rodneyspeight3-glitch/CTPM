# 200 additional app capabilities

These are 200 usable capabilities across reading tools, a private organizer and ten staff workflow packs. Shared editing and export controls behave consistently across workflows. Every listed action is implemented; no demonstration records or external paid connections are created.

Automatic visitor-local day/night is an additional core feature: the default changes at 07:00/19:00 in the device time zone; users can change that schedule or choose Day, Night or Device. Open **Display** on any page. It refreshes while the page is open, follows daylight-saving changes, synchronizes browser tabs and works without location permission. Existing explicit theme preferences are preserved.

Open **Private organizer** in any account or **Operations hub** as an agent/administrator. Organizer screens and APIs are owner-only, including for administrators. Server operators can access the database and its backups. Operational records are staff-only. Purchase approval/rejection is administrator-only; receiving requires an approved request.

## Boundaries and verification

These tools are internal records and plans. They do not place orders, spend money, dispatch workers, create leases, charge deposits or certify legal compliance. Existing property availability, financial history, account roles and private application access remain in their existing workflows. This release preserves the existing SMTP and payment configuration. Email setup can be completed separately.

Records are saved to SQLite. Work records retain property labels when a property is deleted; comments and the most recent 100 activity entries are available on detail views. Personal entries are limited to 1,000 per account and remain private to their owner. Browser reading preferences store no account records or application answers. CSV exports neutralize formula prefixes, and calendar exports retain calendar dates without shifting them across time zones.

Verification: `tests/workbench.test.cjs` checks all ten schemas, permissions, version conflicts, procurement approvals, key custody, property deletion, private exports and recurring tasks. `tests/workbench-browser.cjs` exercises every editor plus comments, exports, print sheets, approval/receiving, private notes, conflicts and phone layouts. `tests/local-theme-browser.cjs` covers multiple visitor zones, daylight-saving folds/gaps, manual choices, storage denial and reading controls. The existing API/browser suites remain part of the release checks.
